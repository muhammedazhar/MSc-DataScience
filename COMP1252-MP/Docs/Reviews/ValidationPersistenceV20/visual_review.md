# Area B persistence through 10 August 2018

The broad lower opening visible on 31 July is still visible on 10 August,
ten days later. The matched 10 m RGB panels show a pale pink-brown block in
the same position, with a similar straight southern edge, eastern boundary,
and narrow connection to the older northern opening. It still spans the
lower eroded interior that was dark blue-green on 6 July. Adjacent cover
remains darker in both the 31 July and 10 August views. Small tone and edge
differences are visible at the native pixel scale. The interpretation rests
on the matching shape and location across the clear observations.

Both dates share the same georeferenced Area B crop, `EPSG:32636` at 10 m
resolution. Full-crop RGB was inspected before the enlarged target contours
and NDVI. The July and August views use matched display limits.
A second assistant viewed the same three panels and agreed on the patch's
location, shape and continued visibility.

The NDVI pattern agrees with what is visible in RGB. Median anchor NDVI across
6 July, 31 July and 10 August was **0.692, 0.314 and 0.292**. In the eroded
interior it was **0.698, 0.299 and 0.266**. By 10 August, all 92 anchor pixels
were below their own 6 July values. These are descriptive measurements of
the frozen target and its 25-pixel interior.

This adds a later clear observation to the provisional **after 6 July and
by 31 July 2018** bracket for Area B's lower expansion. The gap between
observations remains unsampled for the review. Upper and edge pixels were
already mixed on 6 July, and the first-visible date for the original clearing
remains unresolved. Stable-forest control review, complete 27-channel eligibility,
and explicit class approval remain pending. The original group exposure and
all V19 assessments are retained.
