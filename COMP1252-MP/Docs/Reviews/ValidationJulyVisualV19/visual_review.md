# Visual review of the July 2018 openings

Both 6–31 July comparisons show an existing opening that appears to expand.
Area B gives the clearest optical bracket for an expansion. The broad lower
patch becomes visible after 6 July and by 31 July 2018. Earlier pale ground
within both targets leaves the timing of the original clearing unresolved.

## Area A

On 6 July a pale north–south strip is already visible along the western and
upper parts of the target. The central interior is darker blue-green and partly
continuous with the cover to the east. By 31 July the pale rose-grey area is
wider and extends south through the interior. The strongest vegetation-index
decline follows the middle and southern portion of the target. The nearby
pre-existing strip and mixed edge pixels leave the earlier forest extent uncertain.

The four 31 July shadow-risk pixels form a column immediately southwest of
the lower target, in the two-pixel context and outside both the target and the
eroded interior's context. They coincide with a darker flank. The new pale
interior and its NDVI decline occur on distinct, unflagged pixels. The flagged
flank and narrow mixed opening warrant a follow-up before assigning an interval.

## Area B

On 6 July a pale northern bulb connects through a narrow strip to an older
western lobe. The broad lower portion, including most of the eroded interior,
is darker and blends into the surrounding forest-like cover. By 31 July a
contiguous pale pink-brown block fills that lower portion and has a fairly
straight southern edge. The strongest NDVI decline follows the new block,
while nearby vegetation remains relatively green. The full target context is
QA-clear on both dates.

The two observations bound the visible expansion of the lower opening to
**(6 July, 31 July] 2018**. The upper corridor already looks partly open on
6 July, and the exact prior cover near its edges is unresolved at 10 m.
The bracket applies to that new visible patch. Deforestation timing for the
full target and a persistent forest-to-clearing interpretation remain open.
