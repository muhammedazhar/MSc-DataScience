# Site-01 Landsat crop review

Two reviewers independently inspected the matched plain RGB views before
opening the outlined version. The overlays locate the frozen site-01
target, earlier catalogue footprints and the V32 control and context.
The views use Landsat 8 pan-sharpened RGB referenced to the same crop.
The 15 m pan and 30 m multispectral source sampling limit boundary and
canopy interpretation.

The displayed RGB alone cannot distinguish cloud, snow and illumination
effects with confidence. Scene cloud is 56.25% for January and 71.96%
for February at scene scale. The February reference is 14 days after the
proposed Sentinel-2 after image. The date window and the historical forest
class remain unresolved. [January USGS record](https://landsatlook.usgs.gov/stac-server/collections/landsat-c2l1/items/LC08_L1TP_177026_20180112_20200902_02_T1)
· [February USGS record](https://landsatlook.usgs.gov/stac-server/collections/landsat-c2l1/items/LC08_L1TP_178025_20180220_20200902_02_T1).

Keep the 12 January–6 February pair and the fixed alternate control in the
record with an inconclusive reference disposition. Native Sentinel-2
preflight remains pending. The site-01 group is now visually exposed for
future candidate selection. The frozen split remains in force.
