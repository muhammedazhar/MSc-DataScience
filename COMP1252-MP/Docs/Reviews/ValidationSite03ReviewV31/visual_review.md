# Site-03 model-blind review

Two reviewers independently opened both plain RGB boards, then the outlined
boards, then the NDVI boards. They reviewed the same archived views before
consulting any independent imagery. The 10 May observation was used only for
the fixed control.

## Target

On 30 May a long pale pink-brown opening already occupies most of the
proposed target and its eroded interior. On 4 June it remains visible in
roughly the same footprint. The outline of the earlier catalogue polygon
covers the southern and western part of the feature and leaves much of the
already visible opening in the proposed new-loss mask.

On 4 June the opening looks more uniform, with a lighter narrow eastern
margin. The vegetation-index decrease is strongest near that edge. The
western boundary has a narrow band of increase and surrounding areas also
shift in tone. Median target NDVI was 0.388 on 30 May and 0.332 on 4 June,
compared with 0.382 and 0.322 in the eroded interior. The target already had
lower NDVI than its surroundings on 30 May. Edge mixing, modest widening
and differences in the appearance of the scenes remain plausible. The
opening does not establish forest-to-clearing across the five-day pair.
The class and first-visible deforestation interval remain unresolved.

## Proposed control

The main part of the fixed square looks vegetated and stable across the
three observations. A narrow pale north–south strip crosses the western
part of the square. The low-NDVI northwest patch reaches into the square
and its two-pixel context on all three dates. These could be a track and an
open or mixed edge. The whole fixed square is unsuitable as a confirmed
stable-forest control. Its median NDVI was 0.783, 0.825 and 0.769 on the
three dates. The June decrease also occurs in the control context despite
the absence of a visible advancing opening.

The 27-channel inputs and automatic pixel quality checks passed. The
visual evidence excludes this pair and this control from held-out
evaluation. The geographic split stays frozen. The review record marks
site-03 as visually exposed and records zero approved labels.
